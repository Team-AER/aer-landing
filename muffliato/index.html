Keep your words off the transcript.

Muffliato adds a quiet, bounded perturbation to recorded speech. People hear the same words. A speech-recognition model returns an empty transcript.

Talk to us about a pilot How it works
  • Research prototype. The training pipeline is complete; the one-pass model has not been trained or measured against a real recogniser yet.
  • Built against NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, the recogniser behind many meeting and media transcription products.

Illustration of the intended behaviour, drawn from sample text. The waveform and the perturbation are exaggerated for the page; the real budget is a fraction of one percent of full scale.

Where it stands, without rounding up

Per-clip optimisation against Parakeet works. A model that does it in one pass, for speech it has never heard, is the open problem this project exists to solve.

  • 71/90

    Per-clip outcomes decoded empty

    The per-clip oracle, at the quiet budget, on 30 clips from 15 speakers with three restarts each. Fresh-model replay of the saved audio passed.

  • 4.46 PESQ

    Quality at that budget

    Mean PESQ 4.46 and STOI 0.9993 on those outcomes. That is the provisional quality gate, not a listening test; nobody has yet confirmed the change is inaudible.

  • 1.52M

    Parameters in the generator

    One deterministic forward pass, 16 kHz mono in, additive waveform out. No transcript, speaker ID or gradient is needed at inference time.

  • 0/32

    Clips the previous generator suppressed

    The earlier Wave-U-Net design trained to completion and emptied none of its own training clips. This architecture was built to answer that failure. Its own result is pending a GPU run.

Built for rooms where the recording isn’t yours to stop

Blocking the bot and asking for consent cover most meetings. Muffliato is for the recording you cannot prevent: a counterparty who records anyway, a review copy that leaves the building, a voice that is already out there.

  • First

    Confidential meetings in regulated organisations

    Law firms, clinics, boards and universities. Unconsented AI transcription is now live litigation risk, and the notetaker bans already in place stop the invited bot, not the other side’s recorder.

    Who signs: security, general counsel, compliance. Fits beside Zoom and Teams bot blocking and SaaS governance.

  • Second

    Voice talent, narrators and audiobook publishers

    The one adjacent market with published willingness to pay for voice protection. Delivered audio can carry the perturbation so the words are not lifted into training sets or quote farms.

    Who signs: agencies and publishers, with unions as endorsers. Fits beside likeness and takedown subscriptions.

  • Third

    Pre-release review copies

    Screeners and dailies already carry per-copy forensic watermarks. A review copy tolerates an audible-in-principle mark, and the studio controls the encode. No industry standard yet covers AI extraction from these files.

    Who signs: screener and dailies platforms, studio post-production security.

  • Fourth

    Confidential interviews and source material

    Documentary and investigative producers share raw interviews with freelancers and commissioners. Source protection is a legal exposure with a budget behind it.

    Who signs: legal and standards teams at news organisations and commissioning broadcasters.

This order comes from Team AER’s own demand research: observed budget first, observed pain second. Podcast networks, archives and broadcasters were studied and left out; none objected to machine transcription, and several license their speech to AI buyers instead.

Why this, why now

Thirty months of signals show buyers acting against machine transcription itself. Every answer they have today either blocks the bot or asks for consent. None acts on the audio.

One pass through a phase-preserving generator

The model predicts an additive signal in the complex spectral domain, keeps timing and phase, and bounds its output smoothly. It is trained against the recogniser’s exact empty-transcript loss, so the target is the outcome, not a proxy.

  1. 1

    Analyse

    Each utterance is scaled by its own loudness and split into 512-point frames every 10 ms. Real and imaginary coefficients go in together, so nothing about timing is thrown away.

    16 kHz mono · 257 bins · log RMS kept

  2. 2

    Listen locally

    Six gated residual blocks with growing dilation see about 2.5 seconds around every frame: enough for a word and its neighbours.

    dilations 1 to 32 · width 192

  3. 3

    Listen to the whole clip

    A two-layer attention branch at 50 Hz lets the perturbation coordinate across the utterance, then is fused back into the local features.

    4 heads · utterance-wide · offline

  4. 4

    Synthesise

    The head emits an independent complex signal per frame, inverse-transformed and overlap-added. It can place energy where clean speech has none; no low-pass prior is imposed.

    257 real + 255 imaginary per frame

  5. 5

    Bound it

    A scalar bias is solved so the output has zero DC, then a tanh keeps every sample inside the budget. The bound is smooth, so gradients survive training.

    tau 0.00025 quiet · 0.001 diagnostic

  6. 6

    Train on the real target

    Loss is Parakeet’s exact zero-token TDT objective. Per-clip teachers, made on training clips only, bootstrap fitting, then direct fine-tuning takes over. Saved audio is replayed through a fresh model before any result counts.

    teacher, self-distil, direct · replay enforced

What it does not do yet

These are the claims the evidence does not support. Each is a planned experiment or a known boundary, and we would rather you hear them from us.

  • Not measured on a real recogniser

    CPU tests exercise the pipeline with synthetic audio and model doubles. The generator’s suppression rate, on training clips or unseen speakers, is unknown until the first GPU run.

  • One victim model

    The target is Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 with frozen weights and full-context greedy decoding. Transfer to Whisper, streaming decoders or other recognisers is untested.

  • Offline, digital audio

    It operates on recorded files. Live calls that compress or denoise audio can strip the perturbation before it leaves your machine, and playback through a room and a microphone has not been tested.

  • Quiet, not proven inaudible

    The quality gate is PESQ and STOI thresholds. No listening study has been run. Review copies may tolerate an audible mark; delivered masters may not.

  • Machines, not people

    A human can still listen and type. The product narrows automated, bulk extraction; it is not secrecy.

  • Not a legal control

    It does not replace consent, policy or a recording ban, and it is not a substitute for legal advice on any of them.
