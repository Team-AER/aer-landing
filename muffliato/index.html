Your tools are governed. The recorder on the other side of the call is not.

Notetaker bans, allow-lists and consent banners work inside your own tenant. They do nothing about a client’s default-on notetaker, a counterparty that records, or a phone on the table. Once a recording exists, transcription is free and takes seconds.

  • 01

    A privileged client call

    The client joins with an AI notetaker running by default. The firm’s policy bans them; the client’s does not. The transcript now sits in a consumer app with no privilege attached.

  • 02

    A deal negotiation

    Counterparty advisers record the call. Every number, concession and hesitation is searchable text by the time the meeting ends, ready to be run through a model for the weak points.

  • 03

    A board or HR meeting

    A phone on the table, a dial-in from a hotel, an investigation interview. One recording and the discussion is discoverable, leakable and, under wiretap and biometric laws, a claim.

People hear every word. Machines hear nothing.

An audio layer people hear through and models cannot.

The same toolbox as forensic watermarking, with the opposite goal: a watermark is built so a model still hears every word. Muffliato is built so it hears something else.

  1. Step 1

    Protect the call

    Turn on protected-meeting mode in Zoom or Teams: by policy for matter types, deal rooms or committees, or per meeting. Nobody else installs anything.

  2. Step 2

    Speak normally

    An adversarial perturbation shaped to the speech is added live, below what listeners notice. It survives compression, re-encoding and re-recording.

  3. Step 3

    Transcripts fail

    Whoever records, and wherever the file goes, the model’s transcript comes out wrong or empty. Extraction stops before a copy exists, rather than being traced after.

Questions we get asked first.

Can people on the call hear a difference?

No. The perturbation sits below what listeners notice; the call sounds like the call. The demo plays a protected clip first and asks you to spot it.

What about our own recordings and notetaker?

Protected mode is yours to switch on and off. Calls you want transcribed by your own approved tools stay unprotected; calls that must not become text are protected. Policy can decide per matter type or meeting kind.

Which transcription models does it defeat?

OpenAI Whisper, the model behind most consumer notetakers, at 97 to 99 percent attack success in our evaluation. Coverage of further model families is the current research agenda, and each pilot reports results against the tools its participants actually use.

Is it a watermark?

No. A watermark identifies who leaked a copy after the fact and leaves the speech fully readable to a model. Muffliato acts before extraction: the model never gets a usable transcript, so there is nothing to trace.

Does any audio leave our tenant?

No. The protection runs in the meeting pipeline on your side. Muffliato does not receive, store or process call audio.
